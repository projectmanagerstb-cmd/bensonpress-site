Boldly Me: The Boy Beyond the Mirror

Sean T. Benson

At five, Malik folds up a family drawing and hides it in his sneaker because his family doesn't fit the worksheet. At eight, a classmate tells him to cut his curls "like a regular boy." By thirteen, loud voices online are telling him to act tougher, laugh less and never apologize, while his body, his feelings and his friendships all seem to change at once.

Through a grandfather's workshop, an old doorframe, a blue notebook, a dad who is learning to keep his promises, and a boarded-up community center that needs saving, Malik learns a different kind of strength: knowing his worth, owning and repairing his mistakes, caring for the people around him and growing into the man he wants to be without giving up who he is.

Part illustrated story and part coloring journal, Boldly Me follows one Black boy from age five to eighteen. Readers can color the full-page scenes, answer the reflection prompts and make every page their own. A Presence Pledge for fathers, mentors and the grownups who show up closes the book.

You can grow without disappearing.
