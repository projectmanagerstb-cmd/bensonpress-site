Benson Press Training & Workshops: Project Management and Leadership Training for Teams

Sean T. Benson, PMP, LSSBB · 21-year US Marine · former Director of IT PMO, Palomar Health · professor, National University · DBA · author of Establishing A PMO

Project management and leadership training for teams that need measurable results—not more theory. Benson Press Training & Workshops combines modern project-management methods with practical leadership development, guided exercises, realistic simulations and tools participants can apply immediately.

The flagship offering is a two-day Project Management & Leadership Bootcamp (12 instructional hours). The catalog also includes half-day and full-day workshops, a four-week accelerator, leadership cohorts, and customized executive, PMO and train-the-trainer sessions. Typical cohorts are 12–24 participants; delivery can be in person, virtual or hybrid.

Participants practice chartering work, comparing real trade-offs, rehearsing difficult conversations, preparing decision briefs and using AI only as a drafting and analytical aid with accountable human approval. Every offering is designed to produce a workplace artifact, an observed skill demonstration and a 30-day application commitment. The HarborLink case used in the bootcamp is a fictional service-modernization project.

This is original Benson Press instructional material. It is informed by the PMBOK® Guide—Eighth Edition and the PMI Talent Triangle as external reference points; it is not PMI-endorsed.

Written by Sean T. Benson, PMP, LSSBB: 21-year Marine Corps veteran, former Director of IT PMO at Palomar Health, and National University professor.

Audience

Who it's for

  • Delivery teams and new or practicing project managers who need to charter work, plan, manage risk and change, and brief sponsors.
  • Team leads, technical managers and supervisors who lead without (or beyond) formal authority and need influence, coaching and negotiation practice.
  • PMO leaders, analysts and sponsors who want governance, portfolio value, executive decision-making or a sponsor/steering masterclass.
  • Organizations that want a half-day workshop, a full-day lab, the two-day bootcamp, a four-week accelerator, a leadership cohort, or train-the-trainer — in person, virtual or hybrid.

Typical cohorts are 12–24 participants. Programs are described as adaptable for private-sector, government, transit, technology, healthcare, nonprofit and multidisciplinary teams.

It is not a PMP/PgMP/CAPM exam-prep course, not a PMI Authorized Training Partner program, and not a substitute for legal, financial, labor or regulatory advice.

FAQ

FAQ

Is this PMI-endorsed, or does it confer PDUs automatically?

No. The catalog and the README state that this is independent Benson Press training, not a PMI publication, an official PMI course, or evidence of PMI approval. Learners may be able to self-report eligible educational time under current PMI certification renewal rules; clients should verify current PMI requirements. Benson Press does not represent itself as a PMI Authorized Training Partner or as an automatic provider of PDUs.

What is the flagship course?

The two-day Project Management & Leadership Bootcamp (TW-101): 12 instructional hours of integrated casework and an executive/steering simulation, using the fictional HarborLink case.

What other formats are available?

Half-day interactive workshops (3 instructional hours), full-day skills labs (6 hours), a four-week accelerator (four 3-hour labs plus on-the-job practice), a six-session leadership cohort, and customized executive, PMO and train-the-trainer sessions. Delivery can be in person, virtual or hybrid.

How is completion recognized?

The suggested completion standard is at least 80% attendance, submission of required team artifacts, and 70/100 on the capstone rubric. A Benson Press Certificate of Completion can be issued after those checks. It verifies participation in the listed training; it is not a PMI credential, an exam pass, or proof of PMI course approval.

Is HarborLink a real organization?

No. HarborLink is a fictional regional public-service organization used as a teaching case. All figures in the case and in the Excel dashboard's demonstration rows are illustrative.

Do I need special software?

Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint (or compatible apps) to edit and deliver the materials. The Excel workbook uses standard formulas; check them if you import it into another spreadsheet app.

Can the materials be customized?

Yes. The files are editable masters, marked "edit before deployment." The proposal/SOW scope includes adapting the HarborLink case, agenda, terminology and exercises, and validating them against client policy and accessibility requirements. The workbook contains demonstration entries that must be replaced before client use.

Does the training include official PMI content or exam preparation?

No. The content is original Benson Press material informed by publicly cited standards. The SOW lists professional certification or PMP/PgMP/CAPM exam-preparation guarantees, official ATP status, PMI endorsement and automatic PDU approval among the items not included unless explicitly scoped.
