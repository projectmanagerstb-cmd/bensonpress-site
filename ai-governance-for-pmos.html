AI Governance for PMOs: A Practical Kit for Governing AI in Project, Program and Portfolio Work

Sean T. Benson, PMP, LSSBB · 21-year US Marine · former Director of IT PMO, Palomar Health · professor, National University · DBA · author of Establishing A PMO

Your project managers are already using AI. Does anyone know which tools, with what data, and who signs off on the results? AI Governance for PMOs gives a PMO a practical way to answer that in 12 steps: set the mandate, assess maturity, inventory AI use, assign decision rights, set delegation limits with the L0–L5 ladder, tier risk, put a named person behind every consequential decision, map controls, evaluate vendors, pilot, and prove value.

The kit includes a 38-page guide (PDF and editable Word), a 20-tab Excel workbook with working formulas (use-case register, risk and impact assessment, oversight log, controls checklist, vendor scorecard and dashboard), an editable AI use policy, 10 forms and a five-session workshop, a 24-prompt guardrail library, a 14-slide leadership deck and a one-page quick-start.

It is practical guidance, not legal advice, and it does not certify compliance with any law or standard.

FAQ

FAQ

Will this make us compliant with the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001 or the NIST AI RMF?

No. The kit can help you organize the work those frameworks expect: an inventory, risk assessment, human oversight, controls and records. Whether a law applies to you, and what it requires, is a question for your legal team. The guide gives a short, dated summary of each framework and says where to check the current text.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is practical guidance for PMOs. Have counsel review the policy before you adopt it, especially if you handle personal data or use AI in decisions about people.

Do we need a particular AI tool?

No. The kit is tool-neutral. The vendor scorecard uses general categories (enterprise assistants, work-management platforms with AI, agent platforms, model platforms), and you test each option yourself.

Do I need special software?

Microsoft Excel 2016 or later, Word and PowerPoint (or compatible apps). The workbook uses standard functions and also opens in Google Sheets and LibreOffice; check the formulas after importing.

We're a small PMO. Is this too much?

No. Start with the quick-start, the register and the interim red lines, and add the rest as your AI use grows. Step 4 shows how to set up a small council instead of a large committee.

Can I edit the templates and use them at work?

Yes. Every file is editable, and you can adapt it inside your own organization. You may not resell or redistribute it. For several people, use the Team or Enterprise license.

Does it cover AI agents?

Yes. The delegation ladder covers agents that act with approval (L4) and within pre-approved limits (L5), and the controls include a tested stop switch and access revocation.
