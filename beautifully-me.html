Beautifully Me: The Girl Beyond the Mirror

Sean T. Benson

At seven, Maya is told that a boy she likes as a friend must be her boyfriend. At eleven, an edited photograph makes her question the girl she sees in the mirror. By fifteen, everyone seems to have an opinion about who she should be and whom she should love.

Through friendships, family conversations, painful comments, first crushes and the pages of her beloved sketchbook, Maya learns to ask a different question: What do I believe about myself?

Part illustrated story and part coloring journal, Beautifully Me follows a young Black girl's search for belonging, self-love, boundaries and the courage to write her own story. Readers can color the scenes, reflect on their own experiences and make every page their own.
