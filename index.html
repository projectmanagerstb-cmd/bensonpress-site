What we are

What we are

Benson Press is an independent press founded by Sean T. Benson. We publish a small, deliberate list, and we hold every title to the same standard: tell the truth about the people in it, and give the reader a story worth their time.

Stories with purpose

Fiction grounded in lived experience: the discipline, cost, and loyalty of service, told with honesty and craft.

Practical knowledge

Guidebooks for project leaders on establishing and nurturing a Project Management Office.

Voices worth hearing

Books that make room for the reader, including a young Black girl's search for belonging, self-love, and the courage to write her own story.
