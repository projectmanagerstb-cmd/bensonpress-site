The Imprint

About Benson Press

Benson Press is an independent imprint founded by Sean T. Benson. We publish a small, deliberate list, and we hold every title to the same standard: tell the truth about the people in it, and give the reader a story worth their time.

Our first list is the BlackOPS military saga, a three-book story of service, family, and the country those who serve are sworn to defend.

Sean T. Benson

Sean T. Benson is an author, educator, technology leader, and retired United States Marine whose work reflects a lifelong commitment to leadership, learning, service, and personal growth.

Marine and author

Sean T. Benson is a 21-year U.S. Marine Corps veteran and the author of the BlackOPS saga.

As a writer, Sean Benson draws from professional experience, education, military service, leadership, and life lessons to create work that is practical, thoughtful, and grounded in real-world experience.

Project management professional and educator

He is the former Director of IT PMO at Palomar Health. He teaches leadership and project management at National University's School of Business and Economics. His research is on AI governance maturity and AI-supported project-planning decision quality.

Over the course of his career, he has worked across military, government, healthcare, transportation, cybersecurity, information technology, and organizational leadership environments. His experiences have shaped a practical perspective on discipline, resilience, adaptability, teamwork, and the importance of continuing to learn regardless of age, title, or accomplishment.

He holds a Bachelor of Business Administration, an MBA in Project Management, and a Master of Science in Information Technology Management. He is a Project Management Professional (PMP). He is a DBA candidate, with the degree expected in July 2027. He is the author of Establishing A PMO.

He is especially passionate about helping others grow, challenge conventional thinking, and develop the confidence to lead through change.

Outside of writing and teaching, he values family, mentoring, lifelong learning, and creating meaningful work that can help others long after the project, classroom, or conversation has ended.
