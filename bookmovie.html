A new way to experience a book.

BookMovie is designed to be a new publishing format, not merely a trailer or a traditional film adaptation. The goal is to let audiences experience an author's approved literary world as a complete cinematic edition or an ongoing series.

Complete cinematic editions

Purchase an entire author-directed production. The planned price model keeps the experience close to the cost of a digital book.

Ongoing episodic series

Follow your favorite authors and discover new chapters as they are released through an optional series subscription.

The author is the creative director

Two environments. One vision.

BookMovie is being developed to bring creation, publishing and discovery into one connected ecosystem for independent authors, publishers and audiences.

A

Author Studio

An AI-assisted production workspace built around the author's original vision.

  • Import manuscripts and develop scripts
  • Build consistent characters and storyboards
  • Generate, edit and approve cinematic scenes
  • Manage production budgets and publish content
V

Streaming Marketplace

An accessible cinematic library designed for everyday devices.

The proposed BookMovie pilot will demonstrate how an author's literary vision can develop into a cinematic publishing experience.
